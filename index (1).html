Questions people ask first

How do you verify contacts?

Each contact is matched to a company and domain, the role is checked, the address is tested for deliverability, and a person reviews the record before it is added to your list.

How is pricing worked out?

You pay per list, based on the industry, countries and size you need. Send your brief and we will reply with a clear quote.

Can I see a sample first?

Yes. Use the brief builder above and we will send a free sample matched to your target audience.

What format do I receive?

A CSV or Excel file with one row per contact, ready to import into your CRM or email platform.

Can you build a list for an industry that is not listed?

Usually, yes. Describe the audience in your email and we will tell you what we can deliver.
